# KZFR website conventions

- Use verified KZFR wording or plain functional headings. Do not invent slogans or brand promises. The verified tagline is “People Powered Radio.”
- Do not put an individual developer's name or a year in the public site credit. The approved hosting credit is “Hosted by Clyde LLM Factory.”
- Do not decorate navigation and cards with up-right arrows. Ordinary links and buttons are sufficient.
- Keep provider names and implementation details in developer/operator documentation unless visitors need them to make a decision.
- Include the complete currently public recording catalog where available. Do not impose an arbitrary sample limit.
- Preserve provider playback restrictions and keep audio hosted by the station/provider.
- Clearly identify this as a temporary unofficial preview. Do not imply KZFR approval or a production launch.
- Keep credentials, database backups, full scraped snapshots, personal correspondence, and unrelated product code out of this public repository.
- Do not create or use GitHub Issues for tracking. Use the local employment/kzfr coordination and deployment records.

- Preserve a design option close to KZFR’s original cream, tan, white and green appearance. Broken media do not imply consent to replace their identity. Keep the five preview design choices separate from station content.
